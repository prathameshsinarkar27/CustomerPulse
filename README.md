# CustomerPulse – E-Commerce Customer Intelligence Platform

## Project Overview

CustomerPulse is an end-to-end Data Analytics and Business Intelligence project built using the Brazilian E-Commerce (Olist) dataset. The project follows a complete analytics lifecycle, starting from data quality assessment and data cleaning, progressing through exploratory data analysis (EDA), SQL-based business analytics, customer segmentation using RFM analysis, and culminating in interactive Power BI dashboards.

The objective of CustomerPulse is to transform raw e-commerce transaction data into actionable business insights for decision-makers across sales, customer analytics, product management, and regional operations.

---

## Business Objectives

The project aims to answer the following business questions:

### Revenue Analytics

* What is the overall revenue generated by the business?
* How has revenue changed over time?
* Which product categories contribute the most revenue?

### Customer Analytics

* Who are the most valuable customers?
* Which customers are at risk of churn?
* How can customers be segmented based on purchasing behavior?

### Product Analytics

* Which products and categories perform best?
* Which categories generate high revenue despite lower order volumes?

### Regional Analytics

* Which states and cities drive the most revenue?
* Which regions have higher average order values?

### Operations Analytics

* What is the average delivery time?
* How effectively is the fulfillment process operating?

## Customer Segmentation Results

RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) analysis was used to classify customers into meaningful business segments.

### Segment Summary

| Segment             | Customers |
| ------------------- | --------: |
| Champions           |     3,135 |
| Loyal Customers     |     2,894 |
| At Risk Customers   |     4,683 |
| Potential Loyalists |    33,652 |
| Lost Customers      |    51,732 |

This segmentation enables targeted marketing campaigns, retention strategies, and customer value optimization.

## Key Insights

### Revenue Insights

* Total revenue generated exceeded 13.59 million.
* Health & Beauty was the highest revenue-generating category.
* São Paulo was the highest revenue-generating state.
* São Paulo city generated the highest city-level revenue.

### Customer Insights

* Over 51,000 customers were classified as Lost Customers.
* More than 3,100 customers were identified as Champions.
* Loyal Customers demonstrated significantly higher purchase frequency and monetary value.

### Product Insights

* A small number of product categories contributed a significant portion of total revenue.
* High-performing categories consistently generated both high revenue and high order volumes.

### Regional Insights

* Revenue concentration was strongest in southeastern Brazilian states.
* Certain lower-volume states achieved higher average order values than major revenue centers.
