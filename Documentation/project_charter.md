# CustomerPulse Project Charter

## Project Name

CustomerPulse: E-Commerce Customer Intelligence Platform

## Business Problem

The company generates large volumes of e-commerce transactions but lacks a centralized analytics solution to understand customer behavior, sales performance, product performance, regional trends, and delivery operations.

Decision-makers currently do not have a single source of truth for monitoring KPIs and identifying opportunities for growth and operational improvement.

## Project Goal

Develop an end-to-end analytics solution that transforms raw e-commerce data into actionable business insights through SQL, Python, PostgreSQL, and Power BI.

## Target Users

* CEO
* Business Managers
* Sales Managers
* Operations Managers
* Marketing Teams

## Expected Business Outcomes

* Improve visibility into business performance
* Identify high-value customers
* Identify top-performing products
* Monitor regional performance
* Improve delivery efficiency
* Support data-driven decision making

## Scope

Included:

* Sales analytics
* Customer analytics
* Product analytics
* Regional analytics
* Operations analytics
* Dashboard reporting

Excluded:

* Demand forecasting
* Machine Learning models
* Real-time analytics
* Inventory optimization

## Success Criteria

* Interactive Power BI dashboards
* Business KPIs clearly tracked
* Actionable recommendations generated
* End-to-end analytics workflow documented
