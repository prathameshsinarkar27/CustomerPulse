# Business Questions

## Sales Analytics

1. How are sales performing over time?

2. Which categories generate the highest revenue?

3. What are the sales growth trends?

## Customer Analytics

1. Who are the most valuable customers?

2. Which customers are likely to become loyal customers?

3. Which customers are at risk of churn?

## Product Analytics

1. Which products drive revenue?

2. Which products generate the highest profit?

3. Which categories should receive additional investment?

## Regional Analytics

1. Which regions perform best?

2. Which cities generate the highest revenue?

3. Which regions require improvement?

## Operations Analytics

1. How efficient are deliveries?

2. Which regions experience delivery delays?

3. What operational improvements can be recommended?
